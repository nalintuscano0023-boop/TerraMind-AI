🌍 TerraMind AI

An immersive AI powered environmental intelligence and planetary simulation platform.

TerraMind AI is an interactive environmental technology project designed to visualize planetary conditions, ecosystems, climate behavior, wildlife habitats, and environmental insights through a modern, cinematic interface.

The platform combines interactive simulations, environmental visualization, data driven insights, animated ecosystems, and responsive UI into a single experience.


✨ Features

🌍 Planetary Environment Simulation

- 🌅 Sunrise
- ☀️ Day
- 🌇 Sunset

- ☀️ Summer

- ❄️ Winter
- 🌧️ Rain

- 💨 Wind

🌱 Vision

TerraMind AI explores how artificial intelligence, simulation, visualization, and environmental data can work together to create better ways of understanding our planet.

The project focuses on making complex environmental information more:

- Visual
- Interactive
